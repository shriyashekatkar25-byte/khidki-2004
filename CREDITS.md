## Cultural references (research, not imagery)

- *Khidki* (window), *Suru* (start), *Dainandini* (diary), *Aathavani* (memories), *Rangroop* (appearance), *Swagat* (welcome), *Band kara* (switch off) — Marathi words chosen for the app names.
- Tabla thekas for Keherwa (8), Dadra (6) and Teentaal (16) and the note sets of Yaman, Bhupali, Bhairavi and Megh are standard, widely documented Hindustani material. The app calls its melodies "raga-inspired" because it uses only note sets, not characteristic phrases.
- The *nath* sticker follows the Maharashtrian nose-ring silhouette (curved hoop with pearls around a central stone) as a generic form, not a specific jewellery design.
